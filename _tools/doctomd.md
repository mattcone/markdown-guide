[DocToMD](https://doctomd.com) is a web-based converter that turns documents into Markdown. You upload a Word (.doc or .docx), PDF, Excel, PowerPoint, EPUB, HTML, CSV, or JSON file, or paste a public webpage URL, and then preview, copy, or download the Markdown output. There's nothing to install.

Headings, lists, links, simple tables, and images are kept when the source file includes them. Complex layouts, merged cells, and scanned pages may need manual editing, so plan on reviewing the output before you publish it.

The free tier allows three conversions per day without an account (files up to 10MB, PDFs up to 3 pages). Paid passes add OCR for scanned PDFs, ZIP archive conversion, and higher limits.
